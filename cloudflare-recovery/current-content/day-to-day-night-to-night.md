# Day to Day, Night to Night

I have been learning to surrender my day to God as evening comes. Genesis describes the first day with evening before morning ([Genesis 1:5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis%201%3A5&version=ESV)). That rhythm has become precious to me. As the day closes, I give it back to Him and entrust the night to His care.

Psalm 19 says, “Day to day pours out speech, and night to night reveals knowledge” ([Psalm 19:2](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm%2019%3A2&version=ESV)). I hear a rhythm in those words. During the day, I am called to bear His image. As night comes, I surrender to Him and wonder at His creation, trusting Him to reveal what I need to know.

I want Holy Spirit to hold the reins and lead me along the paths where He wants me to go. I may not understand the whole path. I can take the next step with Him, then place the day—and myself—in His hands as evening comes.

From the outside, this way of living might seem odd or peculiar. That is part of what it means to be God’s people: Scripture calls believers “a people for his own possession” ([1 Peter 2:9](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1%20Peter%202%3A9&version=ESV)), called out of darkness to proclaim His excellencies. To me, there is nothing more natural or wonderful than being gently guided by God. I can be among lions or in the midst of fire and still know that He is there. Daniel was kept in the lions’ den, and the three men who would not bow to the king’s image came out of the fire unharmed ([Daniel 6:22](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Daniel%206%3A22&version=ESV); [Daniel 3:24–27](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Daniel%203%3A24-27&version=ESV)). When I believe He has placed me somewhere, I can trust Him there.

That trust lets us hold the things of this life loosely. We can be truly grateful for what we have and still know that what fades is not the final treasure ([Matthew 6:19–21](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew%206%3A19-21&version=ESV)). What is temporal is a dim reflection of the true and lasting things God has promised. That hope can give a person joy even in sorrow.

Most people do not begin by seeing life this way. I didn’t. God has been teaching me that this world is not my home, and I need His wisdom to walk through it. As evening comes, I surrender the day. Through the night, I trust Him to reveal what I need. When morning comes, I rise to bear His image again.
