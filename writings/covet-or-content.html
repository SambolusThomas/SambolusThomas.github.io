Covet or Content?

Have you ever found yourself thinking, I could be at peace if this one thing would change? I have. It is easy to look at the circumstances I am in and let what I can see tell me how much peace I can have.

Isaiah 26:3 says that God keeps in perfect peace the one whose mind is stayed on Him, because that person trusts in Him. I keep coming back to that last part. Where does the peace come from? It comes from God, and my mind stays on Him because I trust Him.

That helps me hear something in Hebrews 13:5–6. The King James says, “Let your conversation be without covetousness; and be content with such things as ye have.” Here, conversation means the way we live. Then God gives us a reason to be content: “I will never leave thee, nor forsake thee.”

I think of coveting and contentment as two directions I can walk. I can spend my time wishing I had someone else’s life, or I can ask God what He wants me to do in the life I am actually living. The longer I dwell on what somebody else has or how different I wish things were, the harder it is to see what God has already provided.

I can ask God to change a hard situation and still be content in Him today. I can take whatever step He shows me and trust Him with the part I cannot see. Sometimes the question I need to ask is simply, Lord, what would You have me learn here?

Romans 8:28 tells us that all things work together for good to those who love God and are called according to His purpose. I often think about that verse in terms of what God is doing for me. Joseph’s story makes me look a little farther. His brothers sold him into slavery, and years later he told them, “Ye thought evil against me; but God meant it unto good” (Genesis 50:20). The good Joseph could finally see reached well beyond his own life. Many people lived because of what God had done through him.

God is eternal. He can see what He is doing in my life and in the lives of the people around me. I see only the little part I am standing in, and sometimes I get in a hurry because that is all I can see.

When I catch myself thinking, I could be at peace if this one thing would change, I want to remember what God has already said. He will never leave me or forsake me. Whatever happens next, the Lord is my helper today.
