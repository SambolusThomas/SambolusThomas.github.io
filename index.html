A Word of Gratitude

An author’s note

The work of Dr. Michael S. Heiser helped set me on this journey. His writing opened questions I had not known to ask and encouraged me to look again at the larger story unfolding through Scripture, particularly its glimpses of the unseen realm.

I regard his work as a blessing from the Lord, and I am grateful for what it stirred in me. As we follow this love story into places his scholarship helped me notice, I want to acknowledge that debt with affection and thanks.

Why I’m sharing this

Why I’m sharing this

I hadn’t planned to make this website. It began quite suddenly, with a desire to share something I have found to be true: God loves us, and His love is worth looking into.

These writings grow out of my own experience, questions, and reading of the Bible. I’m still learning. I don’t have every answer, but I have found something here that has given me hope, and I want to share it.

I’m not selling anything or asking anything from you. My hope is simply that something you read here will help you—whether it gives you encouragement, helps you consider a question, or leads you to open the Bible for yourself.

You’re welcome to take your time.
